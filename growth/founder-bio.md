# Founder bio

## One line
Munene Denis builds the digital rails for Kenya's informal service businesses — starting with DumuWaks, where fundis get customers and get paid safely through M-Pesa escrow.

## Short (≈60 words)
Munene Denis is the founder of DumuWaks and Founder & CTO of Emen Engineering Limited in Nairobi. An Electrical & Electronic Engineer (JKUAT), he builds software, AI and connected hardware for how Kenya actually works: phones, M-Pesa and WhatsApp. DumuWaks connects customers with verified fundis and holds payment in escrow until the job is done.

## Long (≈150 words)
Munene Denis is an Electrical & Electronic Engineer (BSc, JKUAT, 2023) and the founder of Emen Engineering Limited, a Nairobi technology company he started in 2020. His thesis — a receptionist that used face detection and QR codes to check visitors in — set the pattern for his work since: hardware and software designed together for real conditions.

He is now building DumuWaks, a repair and maintenance marketplace where customers find a verified fundi and pay the agreed price by M-Pesa into escrow, released when the job is done. Under the hood is an append-only ledger reconciled daily and payouts that are never sent twice.

Through Emen he has also shipped storefronts, business systems, an AI recipe product, an offline audiobook tool and ESP32 LED-signage firmware, all on infrastructure he runs himself. He holds certifications in Data Science & AI (KIEP-SKIES) and Software Engineering (Power Learn Project).
