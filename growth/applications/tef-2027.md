## Business name and one-line description
DumuWaks — a Kenyan marketplace where customers find verified fundis and pay by M-Pesa into escrow, released when the job is done.

## What problem are you solving?
In Kenya, finding a technician for electrical, plumbing or appliance repairs depends on word of mouth, and paying one is a risk for both sides. Customers who pay upfront risk unfinished work; fundis who work first risk not being paid. Good fundis struggle to reach new customers, and customers stay with whoever a neighbour recommended. In our interviews, [[top finding, with a count from your own interviews]].

## What is your solution?
DumuWaks checks each fundi's ID before showing them as verified, lets customers book by trade and area, and collects the agreed price by M-Pesa into an escrow wallet. The money is released to the fundi when the customer confirms the job, or automatically three days after completion if nothing is reported. Disputes freeze the money until an admin resolves them. An append-only ledger, reconciled daily, keeps every shilling accounted for.

## Who are your customers?
Fundis (electricians, plumbers, appliance repairers) who want more paid work and guaranteed payment; households and small businesses in [[pilot area]] who need repairs from someone they can trust.

## How do you make money?
A 7.5% platform fee (plus VAT), taken from the fundi's share, with a minimum of KES 50 and a maximum of KES 5,000 per job. The customer pays only the agreed price.
