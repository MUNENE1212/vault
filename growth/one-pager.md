# DumuWaks — one-pager (v1)

**A trusted fundi, paid safely.** dumuwaks.co.ke · Live numbers: munene1212.github.io/building.html
Founder: Munene Denis · munene@ementech.co.ke · Nairobi, Kenya

**Problem.** Finding a fundi in Kenya runs on word of mouth, and paying one is a risk for both sides: customers fear unfinished work, fundis fear not being paid.

**Solution.** DumuWaks connects customers with fundis whose ID has been checked. The customer pays the agreed price by M-Pesa into escrow; it is released to the fundi when the job is done. Disputes freeze the money until resolved.

**How we make money.** 7.5% platform fee (+VAT) from the fundi's share, minimum KES 50, maximum KES 5,000. The customer pays nothing extra.
