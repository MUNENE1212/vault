## 2. Before onboarding

## 3. Onboarding a fundi (in person, about 20 minutes)
1. Help them register on their own phone (technician account).
2. Photograph the ID front and back for verification (in the app, never on your own phone).
3. Add their trades, service area and typical prices.
4. Set the payout M-Pesa number and confirm the 6-digit code.
5. Take a profile photo with their consent.
6. Explain escrow in one sentence: *"The customer pays first, the money is held, and it comes to you when the job is done."*
7. Verify them in the admin panel the same day.
8. Add them to the pilot WhatsApp group for updates.

Track in a simple sheet: name, trade, date onboarded, verified (Y/N), first job date.

## 4. Seeding demand
- Hardware shops: ask the owner to recommend DumuWaks fundis to customers asking for one; leave flyers with a QR code to dumuwaks.co.ke.
- Estate WhatsApp groups and Facebook community groups: a short intro post from you (template in outreach-templates.md), with permission from the group admin.
- Referral: first-time customers who book through a referral link or code get [[offer — decide amount]] off; budget [[KES]] for the pilot.
- Your own network: ask 20 people to book their next repair through DumuWaks.

## 5. Weekly operations
- **Daily:** check new bookings; make sure every booking is accepted within [[target]] minutes.
- **After every job:** call or WhatsApp the customer: was it done, was the price fair, would you book again? Log the answer.
- **Friday:** weekly review (weekly-review.md); publish the build-log update.
- **Fix one thing a week:** the issue mentioned most in customer calls.

## 6. Content from the field (with consent)
- One fundi spotlight a week (photo + 3 sentences) for Facebook.
- Film the 3-minute booking demo and the "fundi's day" video in weeks 6–8.

## 7. End of pilot (week 12)
Write a two-page pilot report: numbers per week, repeat rate, escrow share, top 3 problems fixed, 5 quotes, and what the second area needs. This goes into every funding application.
