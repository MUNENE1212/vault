# Customer interviews — 10 fundis, 10 customers (weeks 1–4)

Goal: hear the problem in people's own words before marketing, and collect quotes for the deck and applications. Listen; do not pitch DumuWaks until the last question.

## Before each interview
- 20–30 minutes, in person if possible. English or Swahili, whichever they prefer.
- Consent script: *"I'm building a service to help people find and pay fundis. Can I ask you about your experience for about 20 minutes? I'll take notes. If I use anything you say, I'll leave out your name unless you agree. Is that okay?"*
- Ask separately before recording audio or taking a photo.

## Fundi guide
1. What work do you do, and for how long?
2. How did you get your last three jobs? (Listen for: referrals, WhatsApp groups, hardware shops, walk-ins.)
3. In a normal week, how many days do you have paid work? What do you do on the other days?
4. Tell me about a time a customer didn't pay, or paid late. What happened?
5. How do you agree on a price? Has a customer ever argued about it after the job?
6. How do you get paid today? (Cash, M-Pesa, Till, after or before the job?)
7. Do you use any app or platform for work now? What do you like or dislike about it?
8. What would make you trust a new way of getting customers?
9. *(Last)* If customers paid into a held account and the money came to you when the job was done, what would you think? What worries you?

## Customer guide (households and small businesses)
1. When did you last need a fundi? What for?
2. How did you find them? How long did it take?
3. How did you decide you could trust them?
4. Tell me about a job that went badly. What happened with the money?
5. How did you pay? Before, after, part before?
6. What would you need to see to book someone you have never met?
7. *(Last)* If your payment was held until you confirmed the job was done, would that change anything for you?
